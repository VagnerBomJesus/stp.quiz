Support

Last updated: June 2026

1. How We Can Help

The STP Quiz team is available to answer questions, receive suggestions and investigate any issues. The preferred channel is email.

Support email: vagneripg@gmail.com

2. Before You Contact Us

Many requests are resolved in seconds. Check:

  • App doesn't open or is slow — restart the application;
  • Progress seems incorrect — check Clear Cache;
  • A question seems wrong — report it.

3. Requests We Receive

Questions, Suggestions, Bugs, Content, Privacy and data.

4. How to Report a Bug

Include:

  • Clear description;
  • Steps to reproduce;
  • Expected behavior;
  • Device model and OS version;
  • App version;
  • Screenshot if possible.

5. Content Suggestions and Contributions

We accept new questions, bibliographic sources and factual corrections.

6. Response Time

2 to 5 business days. Data protection requests are prioritized.

7. Privacy of Requests

Messages are treated confidentially and not shared with third parties.

8. Languages

Portuguese, English and French.
